Five things people get wrong, so I will say them plainly

A website is not included in My Studio Books. It is a CRM and I want to keep it true to what it is. Websites are a separate thing, built by me or by you.

My Studio Books does not have a shop where your client logs in and orders prints herself. My Studio Sales is how you sell prints to her — face to face or on a shared screen. Those are two different things and I do not want to blur them.

They are separate programs, not one bundle. The CRM is its own beast. The others were built later, one at a time, because people asked. Some connect to it. None of them are inside it.

There is no trial (the one exception: The Little Album lets you make one free sample album that stays up for 24 hours). Not because I am being difficult, but because people trial a thing, change their mind, and it becomes a mess for everyone.

Launch prices on the programs do not come back. Every program here started lower and has gone up, and it will keep going that way, because the people who got in early have to be honoured. There is no Black Friday price that will beat what is on this page today. A service like a website build is a different thing and can come back on offer — the programs do not.
