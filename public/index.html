Questions

Good to know

Do I need a website or any tech skills?

No. You fill in a form in your dashboard and get a link to share. It works with any website, Instagram, or email.

Can I use photos of my own Santa?

Yes. Upload your Santa's photos and they replace the cartoon Santa everywhere on your page.

What do kids type, and where does it go?

Kids tap answers and type their first name and wishes. Nothing is sent anywhere unless a parent fills in the grown-ups form and agrees to the privacy policy.

How do the daily notes work?

Parents who sign up get one short email from "Santa's Helpers" each morning, December 1 to 24, to read aloud. They can stop them with one click.

Is it really lifetime?

Yes. One payment covers your studio's page every year, as long as A Little Holiday Magic is running.
